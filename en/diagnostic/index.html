Initial project review: terms

72 hours is a target for an initial review of an agreed information package, not an unconditional deadline for a full technical examination of a facility.

When the clock starts

Before work begins, we agree the question, inputs, deliverable and calendar deadline. The review starts after receipt of the agreed package and team confirmation. Missing data, scope changes or access delays require the deadline to be agreed again.

Required inputs

Facility and review objectives; current schedule and milestones; progress information; available drawings and document register; photographs, constraints and client questions. The exact input package depends on the assignment.

What the client receives

A concise management picture based on supplied information, priority risks and information gaps, and recommended immediate decisions. The output format and scope are agreed before the start.

Separate scope

Site visits, instrument-based surveys, verification calculations, full design-document audits and formal expert opinions are not automatically included. Scope, fees, contractors and commitments are set out in an agreed assignment and contract.
