Inquiries and personal data

Who handles inquiries

Inquiries are handled by the CERTUS GROUP team. Website and data contact: info@certusdz.com, +213 551 970 323. Business formation status is described on the company details page.

Data and purpose

The form sends your name, email, phone and task description, plus company, region and project type when supplied. We use these details to respond and discuss the project. Mandatory fields are required to submit; email and telephone remain available alternatives.

Delivery services

When you select Send, your information passes through FormSubmit to the Private Email corporate mailbox. Incoming messages are copied to the website owner’s Gmail. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may process data outside your country. If you do not wish to use this delivery route, please call.

Access and retention

Access is limited to the mailbox owner and authorized team members who need the inquiry to respond. Emails and copies remain in the mailboxes until deleted by the owner; automatic deletion after a fixed period is not currently configured. Inquiries are not published in the site catalog.

Your data requests

To request access, correction or deletion, or withdraw consent, email info@certusdz.com with the subject Personal data and identify the relevant inquiry. Withdrawal cannot undo delivery already completed; requests are handled subject to any applicable mandatory retention.

Technical information

The site stores your language preference in your browser. No proprietary advertising or analytics trackers are installed. Hosting and delivery services may process IP addresses and technical logs for operation and security. Do not submit passwords, financial details or confidential material.
